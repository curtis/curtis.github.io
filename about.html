Hi, I'm Curtis. I've written software professionally since 2001 and picked up Ruby on Rails in 2006, the same year I started this blog. Today I'm the co-founder of {{ site.company.name }}, a Ruby on Rails development company.

What I do

At {{ site.company.name }} we build and maintain custom web applications in Ruby on Rails and JavaScript. We work with early stage startup companies, large enterprise organizations, and everything in between. The work usually looks like one of these:

  • Taking a startup's idea from nothing to a launched product, then adjusting and scaling it as it grows.
  • Joining an existing team to speed up work on a large or legacy Rails app.
  • Maintenance, special projects, and code reviews for apps that are already in production.

Open source

Some of what we build at {{ site.company.name }} is open source:

  • docpdf: a Ruby gem that converts Word, Excel, PowerPoint, HTML, images, and more to PDF, with optional watermarking. I've written about it here.
  • credential_parity: a Ruby gem that fails fast when your Rails encrypted credential files don't declare the same keys.
  • heroku-buildpack-libreoffice: a Heroku buildpack that installs LibreOffice with checksum verification.

Community

In addition to working as a software consulting company, we're also involved in the tech community through Ruby::AZ and CO+HOOTS coworking.

Background

I have an MS in Computer Science from California State University, Fullerton and a BS in Computer Science from the University of Arizona. Before Rails I wrote Java and C++ for distributed systems at Raytheon, and before that I worked on a UML-to-C++ compiler. I've worked at small agile companies and large bureaucratic ones, and found that I like working in and around startups best. That's been my focus since 2006.
